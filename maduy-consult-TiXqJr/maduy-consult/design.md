# Maduy Consult — Public Website Design Notes

## Purpose

The website positions Maduy Consult as an execution-focused advisory firm helping Canadian companies assess, enter and grow in West Africa. The current commercial beachhead is Canadian mining supply and services into Nigeria and Ghana.

## Public architecture

The live public site is intentionally compact:

1. **Home** — positioning, Maduy practice, Alliance overview, Guide, contact
2. **Alliance** — three-firm roles, coordinated delivery model, guardrails
3. **West Africa** — current Canada–Africa trade and mining evidence, Nigeria/Ghana opportunity, source links
4. **Funding** — current CanExport status and funding-readiness guidance

## Content rules

Public content may include:
- current market evidence with named sources
- Alliance structure and role allocation
- verified biographies only
- mining beachhead positioning
- general market-entry and funding-readiness guidance
- legal-services separation language

Do not publish:
- internal pricing or offer ladder
- named prospect lists
- internal risk register
- unverified biographies, titles, office/headcount claims or partner relationships
- assumptions about CanExport or other grant availability
- internal commercial weaknesses or negotiation positions

## Brand

- Ink / dark background
- Orange accent
- Bone / paper neutral backgrounds
- Grey supporting text
- Archivo display type
- Manrope body type

## Primary call to action

**Discuss your West Africa opportunity**

Secondary public CTA:
**Request the Canadian Mining Suppliers' West Africa Market Entry Guide**

## Alliance language

The Alliance is a collaboration of independent professional firms. Legal services are provided solely by Ehinlai Law Professional Corporation under a separate retainer. The specific legal entities engaged for consulting work are identified in the relevant engagement documentation.

## Current data note

Market data should carry a clear update date and be reviewed periodically. The West Africa page was refreshed in October 2026.

## Funding note

CanExport SMEs 2026–27 is closed. The website must not imply that an application can currently be made or that future programme terms will be unchanged.
